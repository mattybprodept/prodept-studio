ABOUT

MATTY
BOURIS

With over two decades in the fashion industry, Matty Bouris is a senior designer and production specialist with a rare combination of creative vision and operational expertise. His career spans menswear, womenswear, activewear, and licensed apparel — working across some of the most recognised brands and licences in global fashion.

From designing menswear at The Upside Sport and leading range development at Insight through General Pants, to four years as Senior Designer at NAR — where he oversaw design and development for Mitchell & Ness, the NBA, NFL, NHL, MLB, NCAA, and McLaren Formula 1 — Matty has built a reputation for delivering at the highest level.

Most recently, he co-founded Something Very Special, directing the brand's full design and production output including a landmark collaboration with Coca-Cola. He now operates independently through PROdept. Design Studios, offering production management, range development, technical design, and brand consulting to fashion labels of all scales.
